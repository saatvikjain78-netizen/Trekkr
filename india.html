Buran Ghati Trek

The Buran Ghati Pass Trek is a diverse 15,000-foot Himalayan crossover trail in Himachal Pradesh that takes you through dense forests, wide meadows, and glacial lakes before a thrilling snow-wall descent.



  • 7 Days
  • Moderate - Difficulty
  • 15,279 ft

Rupin Pass Trek

Rupin Pass is a dramatic 15,250-foot high-altitude Himalayan crossover trek that takes you from Uttarakhand to Himachal Pradesh through changing landscapes like forests, snow bridges, and waterfalls.



  • 10 Days
  • Difficulty
  • 15,100ft

Goechala Trek

The Goechala Trek is a high-altitude mountain trail in Sikkim, India, that brings trekkers face-to-face with the massive eastern face of Mount Kanchenjunga



  • 8 Days
  • Difficulty
  • 14,203ft

Gaumukh Tapovan Trek

The Gaumukh Tapovan Trek is a challenging high-altitude Himalayan journey in Uttarakhand that takes you from Gangotri to the sacred glacial source of the Ganga and up to the majestic alpine meadows of Tapovan right beneath Mount Shivling
